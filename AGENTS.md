# Icon design reviewers

For material changes to fruit icon concepts, use the project color_observer, contrast_observer, botanical_observer, fantasy_fruit_observer, and outline_observer agents when subagents are available. Have them check the actual revised result and resolve substantial conflicts before presenting it. Use cut_open_observer for clicked-state cross-sections, coordinating with botanical and outline reviewers. Use mechanical_observer for proposed physical button construction or plastic relief. These are delegated reviews during work, not always-running background monitors.

The user wants every fruit to be a vivid original alien fantasy fruit, with diverse and highly textured skins. Give each distinct control a different exterior color and silhouette; spread hues evenly across the complete set. Some skins should include subtle gradients or blushes, with the middle options in the linked surface study preferred. A few subtle, healthy-looking sun freckles are welcome on selected fruits, but avoid seed-like pits, bruising, and spoiled-looking spots. Color, contrast and botanical observers should coordinate. Silhouettes should be recognizable yet organically varied, rather than rigid cookie-cutter shapes; outline_observer and botanical_observer should coordinate. Contrasting parts and cut interiors can use varied hue relationships: complementary, neighboring, or other combinations; verify their lightness contrast. The open-folder arrow extends beyond the folder outline. Preserve a flat-backed button concept with a dimensional top, pure black backgrounds, strong contrast, no stems or seeds, and highlights that remain richly colored. Default icons show fruit exterior. Clicking immediately shows two vertically cut halves turned outward roughly 70 degrees, exposing contrasting cross-sections and rind; their outline changes and there is no cut animation. The original exterior's side contours remain visible, and each action has its own distinct cut-state silhouette. Unavailable controls are an explicit color exception: nearly colorless with a large white X crossing and extending beyond the silhouette; the third example in the linked study was preferred. Loading is indicated separately. Controls are icon-only with a descriptive native hover label; the broad complementary-color hover glow may spill over neighboring text. Plastic studies remain archived. See DESIGN.md for the visual references and remaining open questions.
