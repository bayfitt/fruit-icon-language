# Icon design reviewers

For material changes to fruit icon concepts, use the project color_observer, contrast_observer, botanical_observer, fantasy_fruit_observer, and outline_observer agents when subagents are available. Have them check the actual revised result and resolve substantial conflicts before presenting it. Use mechanical_observer for proposed physical button construction or plastic relief. These are delegated reviews during work, not always-running background monitors.

The user wants every fruit to be a vivid original alien fantasy fruit, with diverse and highly textured skins. Give each distinct control a different exterior color and silhouette; spread hues evenly across the complete set. Some skins should include organic splotches or gradients, while others may be uniform; variations must not weaken the outline or contrast. A few subtle, healthy-looking sun freckles are welcome on selected fruits, but avoid seed-like pits, bruising, and spoiled-looking spots. Color, contrast and botanical observers should coordinate. Silhouettes should be recognizable yet organically varied, rather than rigid cookie-cutter shapes; outline_observer and botanical_observer should coordinate. Multipart accents should contrast with their body using complementary or near-complementary hues. The open-folder arrow extends beyond the folder outline. Preserve a flat-backed button concept with a dimensional top, pure black backgrounds, strong contrast, no stems or seeds, and highlights that remain richly colored. Fruit exteriors are the current focus. Plastic and interior studies are saved for later.
