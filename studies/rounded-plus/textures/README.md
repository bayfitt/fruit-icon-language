# Fruit material references

These two original raster textures were generated with the built-in image tool and blended with the Blender materials. The uploaded fruit-reference image was used only to understand different seed arrangements; it is not included or copied here.

Skin prompt: "Create a seamless tileable square ALBEDO texture for a physically rendered original alien fruit skin: close-up of a real ripe waxy tropical fruit peel, rich fully saturated magenta and pink hues, varied subtly like a dragonfruit or berry skin, with fine natural pores, tiny healthy lenticels, soft uneven pebbled cell structure. It must be a flat texture map, uniformly lit, no separate fruit, no cut, no seeds, no leaves, no shadows, no highlight streaks, no white, no gray, no stains, no bruise, no text, no watermark, no border. Fine realistic organic details at several scales, no regular pattern, edges tile seamlessly. The texture is used as an albedo material on a 3D model, not as a complete picture."

Flesh prompt: "Create a seamless tileable square ALBEDO texture of the inside flesh of an original alien fruit: close-up of ripe, moist turquoise/teal tropical fruit pulp, intricate natural translucent-looking cellular and fibrous flesh, organically irregular, vivid saturated cyan and teal hues with modest variations; realistic tissue detail but without any seeds, rind, fruit silhouette or citrus wedge pattern. Flat uniformly lit texture map with no bright white highlights, no dark empty spots, no gray, no shadows, no text, no watermark, no border; tile seamlessly. It is a 3D material input, not a complete picture."

The images are illustrative material sources: seamless tiling, per-pixel saturation and accessibility are not independently established.
